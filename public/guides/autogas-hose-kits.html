Autogas Hose Kits for the GG40: Hose, Pull-Away and Whip

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: A hose kit is everything between the dispenser outlet and the nozzle: a short whip hose, an ARK 19.3 pull-away (break-away) with lanyard, and the long delivery hose, assembled with the nozzle on the end. Propane Nozzle quotes ELAFLEX-based kits built for the GG40. ELAFLEX's LPG hose assemblies for the US and Canada are certified to UL 21 and CSA 8.1-2015, rated 350 psi working pressure with burst above 1750 psi. Lengths and threads

Distributor autogas kits for US dispensers are typically an 18" × ¾" whip plus a ¾" × 12' delivery hose, with the pull-away between them. Other lengths are built to order. Tell us the dispenser model, where the pull-away anchors, the hose length you need and the outlet thread on the dispenser; we confirm the configuration on the quote.

Pull-away

ELAFLEX's ARK 19 separates when the hose is pulled with 200 to 500 N regardless of internal pressure, closes valves on both sides, and can be reconnected by hand. The anchor point must withstand more than the maximum separation force. The 19.3 is the current version ELAFLEX pairs with the GG40.

Replacing an ACME kit

If your dispenser has a GG20 on a ¾" kit today, the GG40 kit uses the same hose connection at the dispenser. Swap the whole kit rather than only the nozzle so the pull-away and hose match the new nozzle's warranty and listing.

Quick answers

What's in an autogas hose kit?
A whip hose, an ARK 19.3 pull-away with lanyard, the delivery hose, and the GG40 nozzle on the end, all assembled. ELAFLEX calls it hanging hardware.
Are the hose kits UL listed?
ELAFLEX's LPG hose assemblies for the US and Canada are certified to UL 21 and CSA 8.1-2015, and the LPG 16 S hose carries UL Classified and CSA marks.
What hose length do I need?
Most US autogas kits use an 18" whip and a 12' delivery hose in ¾". Tell us your dispenser model and where the pull-away anchors and we'll confirm.
