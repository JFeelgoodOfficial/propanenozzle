Installing a GG40: Hose Kit, ARK 19.3 Pull-Away, Thread Sealing and Boot

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: ELAFLEX sells the GG40 as part of a hanging-hardware set: GG40 nozzle, ARK 19.3 pull-away (break-away) and UL-listed LPG 16 S hose. A competent installer connects the nozzle to the hose end with a non-permanent liquid thread sealant (ELAFLEX says no PTFE tape on GasGuard nozzles), tightens with spanners, pressurizes and leak-checks every joint, then hangs the nozzle in an NB-GG boot. The ARK 19.3 separates at a set pull and reconnects by hand.

Who should install it

LP-gas dispensing equipment is installed by competent personnel under NFPA 58 as adopted by your state and your authority having jurisdiction. This page summarizes manufacturer guidance; it does not replace the installer or the manual.

Connecting the nozzle to the hose

  1. Match the hose-end thread to the nozzle inlet (¾" NPT on distributor-listed GG40s).
  2. No PTFE tape. ELAFLEX's GasGuard manual gives two reasons: poor electrical conductivity across the joint, and tape particles breaking loose into the flow path. Use a non-permanent liquid thread sealant.
  3. Tighten with spanners on the flats, never a pipe wrench on the nozzle body.
  4. Pressurize and check every joint with a foaming leak-detection agent.
  5. Run an operational fill on a K15 receptacle and confirm no leaks at the nozzle, the pull-away and the swivel.

Pull-away placement

Mount the ARK 19.3 between the whip and the delivery hose, with its lanyard anchored to the dispenser or a fixing point that can take more than the coupling's maximum separation force. After a drive-off, check both halves and the hose before reconnecting.

Reed-switch dispensers

Some dispensers start the pump from a reed switch in the nozzle boot that senses a magnet in the nozzle. ELAFLEX has not yet published whether the GG40 offers a magnet option; tell us when you order if your boot works that way and we'll confirm.

Quick answers

Does the GG40 fit the NB-GG nozzle boot?
Yes. ELAFLEX states the GG40 is compatible with the existing NB-GG nozzle boot.
Can I use Teflon tape on a GG40?
No. ELAFLEX's GasGuard guidance is to use a non-permanent liquid thread sealant, because PTFE tape conducts poorly and can shed particles into the flow path.
