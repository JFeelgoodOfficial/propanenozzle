GG20 on the Parafour P4-075 Dispenser

Updated 2026-10-07 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: Parafour lists the P4-075 as a single-hose autogas dispenser (its site menu also calls it a slim-line fleet dispenser). Parafour hasn't published which nozzle it ships for, so check the unit before ordering: if the vehicles you fill have 1¾" ACME fill valves and the hose ends in ½" or ¾" NPT, a GG20 fits. Vehicles with K15 quick-connect fill valves need a GG40 instead.

What Parafour publishes

Parafour's dispenser lineup lists the P4-075 as a single-hose autogas dispenser, and its site navigation also calls it the P4-075 slim-line fleet dispenser. Parafour states that all of its dispensers are ETL listed to UL 495, 1203, 1238 and 913 and NTEP certified under NCWM Handbook 44. We couldn't find a published spec sheet that names the P4-075's nozzle, hose size or meter, unlike the P4-100, P4-150 and P4-200, which Parafour describes as built for the GasGuard GG20.

Checking whether a GG20 fits your P4-075

  1. Look at the vehicles' fill valves. 1¾" ACME: the GG20 fits, and its 160 mm reach handles recessed valves. K15 / Euro quick connect, which newer US autogas vehicles use: you need a GG40 or ZVG 2 EURO UL instead.
  2. Look at the hose end. The GG20 swivel comes in ½" NPT (.2) or ¾" NPT (.3).
  3. Check the nozzle boot. If lifting the nozzle starts the pump through a reed switch, order the J magnet option.

If the dispenser already has a nozzle on it, the part number on the nozzle body tells you what was fitted. Send it to us through the quote form and we'll match it.

Quick answers

Does the Parafour P4-075 use a GG20?
Parafour hasn't published the P4-075's nozzle. It's a single-hose autogas dispenser, so a GG20 fits if your vehicles have 1¾" ACME fill valves and the hose ends in ½" or ¾" NPT. K15 vehicles need a GG40.
