ELAFLEX GasGuard GG40: the K15 Quick-Connect Propane Nozzle

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: The GG40 is ELAFLEX's push-on nozzle for the K15 quick-connect fill valves that NFPA 58 has required on new US autogas vehicles since 2020. It is designed to EN 13760, UN R 67 and ISO 19825, flows up to 17 GPM, releases less than 1 cm³ of gas on disconnect, has a lighter lever pull than the GasGuard ACME nozzles, and is UL approved in both latched and unlatched versions. It fits the existing NB-GG nozzle boot and is sold with ELAFLEX's ARK 19.3 pull-away and LPG 16 S hose as a complete hanging-hardware set.

What it is

ELAFLEX PACIFIC introduced the GasGuard GG40 for the US and Canadian markets as a straightforward, recognized nozzle for Quick Connect (K15) propane filling. Where the GasGuard GG1 and GG20 screw onto a 1¾" ACME thread, the GG40 pushes onto the K15 receptacle and locks. ELAFLEX built it to carry the GasGuard reputation for reliability and user comfort into the K15 era.

How K15 filling works

The operator pushes the nozzle over the vehicle's K15 fill valve until the locking segments engage, then pulls the lever. The GG40 uses larger locking segments for a secure connection. The design gives low effort at the lever, lower than ELAFLEX's own ACME nozzles, and releases less than 1 cm³ of propane when the nozzle comes off. ELAFLEX states that personal protective equipment is not necessary for the GG40 unless local codes or regulations require it.

Who it is for

  • Fleets and school-bus operators fueling vehicles built or converted since 2020, which carry K15 valves.
  • Autogas stations replacing ACME hose ends as their customers' vehicles turn over.
  • Dispenser builders specifying hanging hardware for new US installations.

If you still fill forklift cylinders, RVs or older ACME vehicles, you need an ACME nozzle such as the GG20 for those. See GG40 vs GG20.

Quick answers

What is the ELAFLEX GasGuard GG40?
ELAFLEX's push-on propane nozzle for K15 quick-connect fill valves, designed for the US and Canada. It flows up to 17 GPM, releases under 1 cm³ on disconnect, and is UL approved in latched and unlatched versions.
Is the GG40 UL listed?
ELAFLEX states that both the latched and unlatched versions of the GG40 are UL approved, and the nozzle carries the UL Classified mark in ELAFLEX's brochure.
What flow rate does the GG40 deliver?
Up to 17 GPM, about 64 L/min, per ELAFLEX. Field flow depends on the pump, hose and vehicle valve.
