Will a GG20 Work With My Dispenser? Compatibility Checklist

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: A GG20 fits any LPG dispenser whose hose ends in a ½" or ¾" NPT male thread and runs within the nozzle's 25 bar (362 psi) rating, as long as the thing being filled has a 1¾" ACME fill valve. Parafour designs its P4-100, P4-150 and P4-200 dispensers for the GG20. Check three things: the fill valve style, the hose-end thread, and whether the dispenser starts its pump from a reed switch in the nozzle boot (then order the J magnet option).

The GG20 is rated for 25 bar (362 psi) working pressure and LPG only (propane, butane and mixtures). It is UL 125 listed. Your installer and authority having jurisdiction have the final say on any dispenser installation.

Dispensers known to ship for the GG20

Parafour

Parafour describes three of its dispensers as built for the GasGuard GG20: the P4-100 (one autogas hose), the P4-150 (one autogas hose plus a cylinder hose) and the P4-200 (two autogas hoses). It also lists a single-hose P4-075 without a published nozzle spec. Each guide covers which GG20 to order for that unit.

Gilbarco-based autogas dispensers

A 2016 Houston-Galveston Clean Cities propane presentation lists a Gilbarco-based autogas dispenser with the ELAFLEX GasGuard GG20 nozzle as standard equipment. Configurations vary by builder, so check the hose-end thread on yours.

Other dispensers

If your dispenser isn't named here, check the three items above against its manual or spec sheet. The dispenser maker can confirm the hose-end thread and whether the boot uses a reed switch. Send us the make and model through the quote form and we'll check before you order.

Filling cylinders from the same dispenser

Forklift cylinders with 1¾" ACME fill valves are exactly what the GG20's long reach is for, so on a dual-hose unit like the P4-150 the GG20 can serve both jobs. Follow the cylinder-filling sequence in how to fill forklift cylinders and RVs.

Quick answers

Will a GG20 work with a Parafour P4-150?
Yes. Parafour designs the P4-150 for the GasGuard GG20 on its autogas hose. Match the GG20 inlet (½" or ¾" NPT) to the hose kit, and ask whether the boot uses a reed switch, which needs the J magnet option.
Will a GG20 fit my dispenser?
If the hose ends in ½" or ¾" NPT, the system stays within 25 bar (362 psi), and you are filling 1¾" ACME valves, yes. Reed switch boots need the J magnet option. K15 quick-connect vehicles need a GG40 instead.
Do I need an adaptor to use a GG20 on my dispenser?
No, and ELAFLEX advises against adaptors at the fill point. Choose the inlet size that matches your hose end instead.
