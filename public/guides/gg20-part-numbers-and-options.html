GG20 Part Numbers Decoded: Nose Pieces, Inlets and Option Codes

Updated 2026-10-07 · Sourced from ELAFLEX documentation

Short answer: A GasGuard 1¾" ACME part number is built from nozzle style (GG1 or GG20), nose piece (E, H or DN), then options: J magnet, S strainer, L hold-open latch, followed by inlet size. In the ELAFLEX catalogue the GG20 types are GG20.2 / GG20.3, GG20H.2 / GG20H.3 and GG20DN.2 / GG20DN.3, where .2 is ½" NPT female and .3 is ¾" NPT female. Extra suffixes add J (magnet), L (latch), G (splashguard) or B (hard-anodized connector with brass ACME insert). Distributors don't all write these codes the same way, so confirm nose piece, latch and inlet in words before ordering.

How ELAFLEX builds the number

ELAFLEX's Information 6.15 lays the code out in six positions:

#PositionChoices
1Nozzle styleGG1 (125 mm reach) or GG20 (160 mm reach)
2Nose pieceE (single, attended, highest flow), H (hybrid, lower lever force), DN (dual, unattended/public)
3MagnetJ, only if the dispenser uses a reed switch in the nozzle boot
4StrainerS, standard since July 2014
5LatchL, optional hold-open latch
6Inlet size½" (15 mm) or ¾" (20 mm) NPT female

How US distributors write it

Codes drift between catalogues. Examples seen in US distributor listings: "GG20S" (with strainer), "GG20SL" (strainer and latch), "GG20DNS", and a listing titled simply "GG20" that is described as having a locking latch. Because the latch changes the UL status and the nose piece changes who may safely use it, confirm three things in plain words on every quote: nose piece (single, hybrid or dual), latch (yes or no), inlet (½" or ¾").

Quick answers

What does .3 mean in GG20.3?
A ¾" NPT female inlet swivel. A .2 suffix means ½" NPT female.
What does the L suffix mean on a GasGuard nozzle?
A hold-open latch. ELAFLEX notes the latch is not covered by the UL listing and isn't permitted everywhere.
