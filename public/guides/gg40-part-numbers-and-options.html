GG40 Part Numbers and Options: Latched, Unlatched and Inlet

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: GasGuard codes follow a pattern: nozzle style, then options, then inlet. Distributors list the latched GG40 as GG40SL (S for strainer, L for hold-open latch) with a ¾" NPT inlet; the unlatched nozzle drops the L. Both versions are UL approved. ELAFLEX has not published a full GG40 code table yet, so confirm three things in words on every order: latch (yes or no), inlet thread, and whether you need a matching hose kit.

Which version

Pick latched where a trained operator stays with the nozzle and fills many vehicles a shift; the hold-open latch takes the strain off the hand. Pick unlatched where your authority having jurisdiction or site policy wants an attended lever. Because both carry UL approval, the choice is operational rather than a compliance question, which is a change from the ACME GasGuards.

Spares and repair

ELAFLEX describes the GG40 as low maintenance and simple to repair, with an 18-month manufacturer's warranty. A GG40 spare-parts list has not been published in the material we cite; ask for the service manual with your quote if you plan to repair in-house.

Quick answers

What does GG40SL mean?
GasGuard GG40 K15 nozzle with strainer (S) and hold-open latch (L), listed by US distributors with a ¾" NPT inlet.
Is the latched GG40 UL listed?
Yes. ELAFLEX states both the latched and unlatched GG40 are UL approved.
