GG40 Maintenance, Repair and Warranty

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: ELAFLEX describes the GG40 as low maintenance and simple to repair, with an 18-month manufacturer's warranty, and says the robust design is built for a longer lifetime. ELAFLEX has not yet published a GG40 inspection schedule or seal-kit number in the material we cite, so follow your site's LP-gas inspection routine, store the nozzle in its NB-GG boot, and ask for the GG40 service manual with your quote if you plan in-house repair.

What ELAFLEX says

  • Low maintenance, simple to repair, which ELAFLEX summarizes as low running cost.
  • Light weight and a robust design for a longer lifetime.
  • 18-month manufacturer's warranty.
  • Store in the NB-GG nozzle boot, which encloses the nozzle so residual gas can't enter the dispenser housing.

Daily checks that apply to any LPG nozzle

  • Nozzle nose and locking segments clean and undamaged; no debris in the K15 interface.
  • Swivel turns freely; no gas smell at the hose connection or pull-away.
  • Lever and latch move freely and return.
  • Hose free of cuts, kinks and abrasion; lanyard intact on the pull-away.

If a nozzle leaks, sticks or won't lock onto a valve, take it out of service and have a trained LP-gas technician inspect it. ELAFLEX states it cannot be responsible for the performance of repaired nozzles, so use genuine parts.

Inspection interval and seal kit

ELAFLEX has not yet published a GG40 inspection schedule or seal-kit part number in the documents we cite. For its ACME nozzles ELAFLEX recommends a full inspection every 6 to 12 months and a seal kit every 24 months; treat that as a starting point for your site routine until GG40-specific guidance is available.

GG40 figures on this page come from the ELAFLEX PACIFIC GG40 brochure (2025) and distributor listings. ELAFLEX has not published every number for the GG40 yet; where a figure is missing we say so instead of guessing. Confirm the current data sheet with your quote.

Quick answers

What warranty does the GG40 have?
18 months, manufacturer's warranty, per ELAFLEX.
How often should a GG40 be inspected?
ELAFLEX hasn't published a GG40 schedule yet. Its ACME GasGuards get a full inspection every 6 to 12 months and a seal kit every 24 months; use that as a starting point and follow your site routine.
