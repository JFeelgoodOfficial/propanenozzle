Will a GG40 Work With My Dispenser? K15 Hose Kits on Parafour and Others

Updated 2026-10-16 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: A GG40 fits any autogas dispenser whose hose kit ends in the nozzle's inlet thread (¾" NPT on distributor-listed GG40s) and whose boot is an NB-GG or similar. The dispenser doesn't care which nozzle hangs on it; the vehicles do. Parafour's P4-100, P4-150 and P4-200 are documented with the GG20 on a ¾" hose kit, so a K15 kit with a GG40 goes on the same hose connection; ask the dispenser supplier for the K15 version of the kit.

The three things that decide fit

CheckGG40 needs
Hose-end threadMatches the GG40 inlet: ¾" NPT per distributor listings. Have the hose kit made with the right end rather than adding reducers.
Pull-awayAn ARK 19.3 or equivalent break-away between whip and delivery hose; the GG40 ships as part of ELAFLEX's set with it.
Boot and pump startNB-GG boot fits. If the boot starts the pump with a reed switch, ask us whether your GG40 needs a magnet option (not yet published; we confirm on the quote).

Parafour

Parafour describes its P4-100, P4-150 and P4-200 as designed for the GasGuard GG20 on a ¾" autogas hose kit sold separately. The hose connection on the dispenser is the same whichever GasGuard nozzle is on the end of it, so a K15 hose kit with a GG40 fits the autogas hose position; the cylinder hose on a P4-150 keeps its cylinder nozzle. Ask Parafour or your distributor for the K15 version of the hose kit, or send us the dispenser model through the quote form and we'll quote the GG40 and a matching kit.

Other dispensers

Check the three items above against the dispenser's manual or spec sheet, and confirm the hose-end thread with the dispenser maker. Any dispenser built for a GasGuard ACME nozzle can carry a GG40 instead once the vehicles it serves are K15.

GG40 figures on this page come from the ELAFLEX PACIFIC GG40 brochure (2025) and distributor listings. ELAFLEX has not published every number for the GG40 yet; where a figure is missing we say so instead of guessing. Confirm the current data sheet with your quote.

Quick answers

Will a GG40 work with a Parafour P4-150?
Yes, on the autogas hose. Parafour documents the P4-150 with a GasGuard nozzle on a ¾" hose kit; a K15 kit with a GG40 fits the same connection. Ask your distributor for the K15 version of the kit.
