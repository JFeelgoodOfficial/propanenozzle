LPG Dispenser Kit Parts: NB-GG Nozzle Boot, Sleeves and Nozzle Guards

Updated 2026-11-11 · Sourced from manufacturer documentation

Short answer: ELAFLEX draws the same kit around every LPG nozzle: a nozzle boot on the dispenser (NB-GG for GasGuard nozzles, NB-ZVG for ZVG nozzles), a KS 16 anti-kink sleeve and a CS 16 colour sleeve at the nozzle end of the hose, and a BS 19 break sleeve over the ARK 19 safety break. Three nozzle options are described as guards: a magnet built into the nozzle guard for reed-switch dispensers, a soft PVC splashguard on the GasGuard ACME and bayonet nozzles, and the EA 866 scuffguard on ZVG 2 nozzles. The GG40 fits the existing NB-GG boot.

Nozzle boots

A boot is the enclosed holster the nozzle hangs in between fills. ELAFLEX makes two for LPG: the NB-GG for GasGuard nozzles and the NB-ZVG for the ZVG 2 and ZVG 1, which ELAFLEX says also takes nozzles of other manufacturers. In its GG40 brochure ELAFLEX states the GG40 is compatible with the existing NB-GG boot, so a dispenser already set up for a GasGuard ACME nozzle keeps its boot when it moves to K15.

Guards: three different things

ELAFLEX uses the word guard for three unrelated options. All three are factory options on the nozzle, ordered by a suffix on the part number.

Reed-switch dispensers and the GG40

Some dispensers start the pump when a reed switch in the boot senses a magnet in the nozzle. The catalogue lists a magnet option for every GasGuard ACME and bayonet nozzle and for the ZVG 2 range. ELAFLEX has not yet published whether the GG40 has one. If your boot works that way, say so when you ask for a quote and we will confirm it before you order.

Quick answers

What is the ELAFLEX NB-GG?
The nozzle boot for GasGuard nozzles: the holster the nozzle hangs in on the dispenser. ELAFLEX states the GG40 is compatible with the existing NB-GG boot.
What is the difference between the NB-GG and NB-ZVG nozzle boots?
NB-GG is for GasGuard nozzles. NB-ZVG is for the ZVG 2 and ZVG 1, and ELAFLEX says it also takes nozzles of other manufacturers.
What is the KS 16?
ELAFLEX's anti-kink sleeve for 16 mm LPG dispenser hose, fitted where the hose leaves the nozzle. It is listed on catalogue page 211.
What does the magnet in the nozzle guard do?
It lets a reed switch in the dispenser sense the nozzle and start the pump without contact. The option code is J on GasGuard ACME and bayonet nozzles and M on ZVG 2 nozzles.
What is a GasGuard splashguard?
An orange soft PVC guard that protects the user's hands against any gas release after the nozzle valve has shut. It is an option on the GG1, GG20 and GG30.
What is the EA 866 scuffguard?
A black or orange PU / PA cover for ZVG 2 nozzles that carries the LPG fuel grade identification to EN 16942 and a printing space.
