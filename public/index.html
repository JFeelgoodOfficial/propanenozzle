In short: the ELAFLEX GasGuard GG20 is a UL 125 listed LPG nozzle with a 1¾" ACME coupling and a 160 mm connector nut, built for filling forklift cylinders, RVs and small tanks whose filler valves sit too deep for a standard nozzle. It flows up to 63 L/min (about 16.6 gpm) at 12 bar, is rated to 25 bar (362 psi), weighs about 2.0 kg (4.4 lb), and comes in three versions: GG20, GG20H and GG20DN. Propane Nozzle quotes all three for US delivery.

Why a long-reach nozzle

Stop forcing a short nozzle onto a buried valve

A standard 125 mm ACME nozzle can bottom out against the cylinder collar or bracket before the threads fully engage. Operators compensate by angling the nozzle, cross-threading, or reaching for an adapter. ELAFLEX's own manual advises against fill point adaptors.

The GG20 adds 35 mm of reach to the connector nut and a guided, extended thread that lines the nozzle up with the fill point before it engages. Everything behind the coupling (valve, nose pieces, flow path) matches the shorter GG1 series, so crews don't learn a new tool.

Built-in safety

What happens when someone gets it wrong

Pull the lever while the nozzle isn't coupled and nothing comes out; the GG20 can't discharge LPG to atmosphere uncoupled. If the fill point gasket is missing, the nozzle still seals. A 300 µm (50 mesh) strainer ships fitted as standard to catch debris before it reaches the tank.

The connector is a high-strength aluminum casting with a stainless steel ACME insert, so it resists the thread distortion that ends the life of cheaper nozzles. When seals do wear, the whole nozzle is repairable.

    GasGuard GG20 FAQ

    What is the ELAFLEX GasGuard GG20?
    A 1¾" ACME LPG dispensing nozzle made by ELAFLEX PACIFIC with a 160 mm connector nut. The long reach lets it couple to deep-seated filler valves, such as those on forklift cylinders and recreational vehicles, that a standard-length nozzle can't reach.
    What's the difference between the GG20 and the GG1E?
    Reach. The GG20 connector nut is 160 mm long, 35 mm longer than the 125 mm GG1E. Flow and pressure ratings are otherwise the same. Choose the GG20 when the fill valve sits recessed in a bracket, cradle or body panel.
    GG20, GG20H or GG20DN: which should I buy?
    GG20 for trained operators where cost matters most. GG20H if operators fill all shift and lever fatigue is a complaint. GG20DN anywhere the public or untrained staff fill, because its dual nose piece seals even when the nozzle isn't fully tightened. See the comparison table.
    Is the GG20 UL listed?
    Yes, all three versions are listed to UL 125. ELAFLEX notes the optional hold-open latch is not covered by that listing.
    What flow rate does it deliver?
    ELAFLEX rates the GG20 at 63 L/min (about 16.6 US gpm) at 12 bar system pressure, and the GG20H and GG20DN at 60 L/min (about 15.9 gpm). Your pump, hose and the vehicle's fill valve set the real-world number.
    What hose connection does it take?
    A stainless steel inlet swivel in ½" NPT female (part suffix .2) or ¾" NPT female (suffix .3). Ask about matching hose, pull-away and nozzle boot when you request a quote.
    Does it seal if the fill valve gasket is missing?
    ELAFLEX states it does, and that the nozzle cannot release LPG if the lever is pulled while uncoupled.
    Does the GG20 replace the old LG20?
    Yes. The GG20 range supersedes the GasGuard LG20.
    How often should it be inspected?
    The ELAFLEX operating manual for GG1 and GG20 nozzles recommends inspection every 6 to 12 months and a new seal kit every 24 months. See the inspection schedule.
