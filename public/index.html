The K15 rule

Why your next nozzle pushes on instead of screwing on

NFPA 58 requires the K15 quick-connect refueling valve on autogas vehicles produced or converted after 1 January 2020. It is not retroactive: an older vehicle keeps its 1¾" ACME valve until it is converted or replaced. But every new bus, van and pickup coming into a fleet carries K15, so the hose end at the dispenser has to change with it.

The K15 connection releases a fraction of the propane an ACME disconnect does. ELAFLEX rates the GG40 at under 1 cm³ per disconnect; its ACME GG20 releases 1.9 cm³. Industry groups put the overall reduction at about three quarters, a trade figure rather than an independent test.

How the GG40 fills

Push on, lock, fill

Push the nozzle onto the K15 receptacle until the locking segments engage, then pull the lever. The GG40's larger locking segments hold the connection, and the lever takes less effort than ELAFLEX's own ACME nozzles. Latch it for attended fleet fueling, or run it unlatched; both versions carry UL approval.

ELAFLEX states personal protective equipment is not necessary with the GG40 unless local codes or regulations require it. Follow your site rules.

Questions buyers ask

GasGuard GG40 FAQ

What is the ELAFLEX GasGuard GG40?
ELAFLEX's push-on propane nozzle for K15 quick-connect fill valves, designed for the US and Canadian markets. It flows up to 17 GPM, releases under 1 cm³ on disconnect, has a lighter lever pull than the GasGuard ACME nozzles, and is UL approved in latched and unlatched versions.
Do I need a GG40 or a GG20?
Look at the fill valve. K15 quick-connect, required on autogas vehicles built or converted since 1 January 2020: GG40. 1¾" ACME thread, on older vehicles, forklift cylinders and RVs: an ACME nozzle such as the GG20, which we no longer sell. See GG40 vs GG20.
Is the GG40 UL listed?
ELAFLEX states both the latched and unlatched GG40 are UL approved, and the nozzle carries the UL Classified mark in ELAFLEX's brochure.
Latched or unlatched?
Latched for attended fleet fueling where the operator stays with the nozzle; the hold-open latch spares the hand. Unlatched where your authority having jurisdiction or site policy wants an attended lever. Both are UL approved, so it is an operational choice.
What is in a hose kit?
A whip hose, an ARK 19.3 pull-away with lanyard, the LPG 16 S delivery hose and the GG40, assembled. ELAFLEX's LPG hose assemblies for the US and Canada are certified to UL 21 and CSA 8.1. See the hose kit section.
Does the GG40 fit my existing nozzle boot?
Yes. ELAFLEX states the GG40 is compatible with the existing NB-GG boot used for its ACME GasGuard nozzles.
Will it fit my dispenser?
If the hose kit ends in the GG40's inlet thread (¾" NPT on distributor-listed nozzles) and the boot is an NB-GG or similar, yes. Parafour's P4 dispensers take the same ¾" hose connection. See dispenser compatibility.
Can I use an adapter to fill ACME vehicles with a GG40?
Adapters exist, but NFPA guidance and the trade press treat them as a stopgap, and ELAFLEX advises against fill-point adapters. Fit the nozzle that matches the valve.
What warranty does it have?
18 months, manufacturer's, per ELAFLEX.
