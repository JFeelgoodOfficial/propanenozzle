Privacy

Updated 2026-11-11

Propane Nozzle is an independent reseller of ELAFLEX GasGuard nozzles and hose kits. This page lists what the site collects and why.

Quote form

When you send the quote form we store what you typed (name, company, email, phone, product choices and notes) together with how you found the site: the first page you landed on, the referring site, any campaign tags in the link you followed, a Google Ads click id if there was one, the Google Analytics client id, and the number of pages you viewed. We use it to answer your request and to learn which pages and campaigns bring enquiries. It is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and is not sold or shared for marketing.

Chat

Questions typed into the chat are sent to Groq, Inc., which runs the language model that answers them, along with the relevant excerpts from our guides. If you type an email address into the chat, we store that message as an enquiry so we can reply. Don't type anything into the chat you wouldn't put in an email.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons lead to quote requests. It sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*) and sends Google your browser and device type, approximate location derived from your IP address, the pages you view, the site that sent you, and events such as opening the chat, starting the quote form or clicking an email link. We don't send Google your name, email, phone number, the contents of the quote form or the text of chat questions.

Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off. Google's own policy is at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. To opt out on every site, install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or block cookies for this site.

This browser

The site keeps two small items in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme choice, and the first page and campaign tags of your first visit (used only to attribute a quote request). Clear site data in your browser to remove them.

Contact

To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, email sales@propanenozzle.com.
